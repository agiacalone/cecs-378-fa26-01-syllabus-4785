# Discussion Forum — Rules and Grading

*Applies to CECS 378 §01, Fall 2026. Part of the course syllabus; see
[the syllabus README](https://github.com/agiacalone/cecs-378-fa26-01-syllabus-4785/blob/main/README.md) for how it fits into the grade.*

Participation is **10% of your course grade**, and it is assessed entirely
through the discussion forum on Canvas. Each week's prompts go out with that week's
readings, and they are about those readings — the forum is where the reading
gets discussed, in place of the reading questions previous semesters turned in.

This page is what "participation" means in concrete terms, so that neither of us
has to guess at the end of the semester.

## What earns credit

A post earns credit when it **moves the conversation**. At least one of these:

- **Answers the prompt with a reason.** Not just *what* you think, but *why*. Prompts are tied to that week's readings, so engaging with what you read is the shortest path to a post that earns credit.
- **Brings evidence.** A citation, a manual page, a benchmark, a code snippet you
  actually ran, a counter-example.
- **Responds to a specific classmate.** Quote or name the point you are
  responding to. Extend it, complicate it, or disagree with it.
- **Asks a question someone can answer.** Real confusion, stated precisely
  enough to be useful, counts. "I don't get it" does not.

Short posts are fine. A well-aimed three sentences beats a page of restatement.

## What does not earn credit

- Agreement with no addition: "Great point!", "I agree with the above."
- Restating the prompt or the lecture slides back at us.
- A wall of text that never commits to a claim.
- Posting all semester's contributions in the last week. ==Discussion is a
  conversation, and a conversation has a time in which it happens.==
- **Slop.** Grammatical, on topic, plausible, says nothing. This is the most
  common way to score zero, and a model will produce it for you on request.
  ==If you cannot point to the sentence someone could disagree with, it is
  slop.==
- Anything generated wholesale and posted as your own. See below.

## Conduct

Disagree with the argument, not the person. You are talking to classmates who
will be your colleagues in industry within a couple of years, in a written record
that persists all semester.

- **Attack claims, never people.** "That breaks when two threads race" is
  criticism. "You clearly didn't read" is not.
- **Assume competence.** Everyone here is mid-way through learning this material,
  including whoever seems most confident.
- **No harassment, slurs, or personal attacks**, in any direction. This is not a
  participation-grade matter; it is a Student Conduct matter and will be handled
  as one.
- **Keep it on topic.** Course material, course tools, course questions.

## Academic integrity in the forum

The course [Academic Honesty](https://github.com/agiacalone/cecs-378-fa26-01-syllabus-4785/blob/main/README.md#academic-honesty) and
[AI](https://github.com/agiacalone/cecs-378-fa26-01-syllabus-4785/blob/main/README.md#ethical-use-of-artificial-intelligence-large-language-models)
policies apply to discussion posts exactly as they apply to code and exams.
In this context specifically:

- **Cite what you did not write.** A link is enough for a web source; quote marks
  for a quoted sentence.
- **AI-assisted is fine, AI-substituted is not.** Using a model to check your
  reasoning or tighten your wording is permitted, and must be disclosed. Pasting
  a generated answer as your own understanding is not.
- **None of this depends on detection.** No checker, no accusations. Generated
  posts fail on their own: ==they agree with everyone, cite nothing, and never
  name a classmate.== A post with a claim and a reason clears the bar no matter
  what helped you write it.
- **Do not post solution code** to an open assignment. Discussing an *approach*
  is encouraged; handing over a working answer is sharing code, which the
  syllabus already defines as cheating.

## Grading

Participation is scored on **substance, not volume**. There is no post quota to
grind out. Prompts, cadence, and due dates are posted on Canvas; the quality bar
is this page.

> **No makeups.** Participation cannot be made up after the fact, because a
> discussion that has ended cannot be participated in. A semester of silence
> forfeits the 10%; there is no alternative assignment.

If you are unsure whether something would count, ask during office hours or post
the question in the forum — asking that question in the forum is itself
participation.

## Accessibility

If any part of the forum format is a barrier for you, contact me and the
[Bob Murphy Access Center](https://www.csulb.edu/bmac) early. Accommodations are
straightforward to arrange and impossible to apply retroactively.
